Between "we have an idea" and an application that runs in daily work, there used to be workshops, specifications and weeks. The app builder shortens that path to four steps. Control stays with you.

## The four steps

- **Idea**: You describe in plain language what the process should do.
- **Process description**: From that, a structured description emerges, with the people involved, the steps, the decisions and the data.
- **Process diagram**: The description is rendered as a diagram. This is where you first see whether your process was understood correctly.
- **Running app**: From the approved diagram, an application is generated on the platform, including roles, data and an interface.

## Why every step stays visible

No step runs blind. The description and the diagram can be reviewed and corrected before the next one starts. The diagram is the most honest checkpoint: a misunderstanding shows up there, while it still costs little. The AI generates. The decisions stay with you.

## Where to start?

Take a process you could explain to someone in five minutes. An approval, a data entry, a recurring report. Those are exactly the cases that suit a first run. Guided, this happens in the "Vibe your Issue" format. On your own, directly on the platform.
