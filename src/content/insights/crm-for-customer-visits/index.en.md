Knowing the last order and the revenue of the past months before you even ring the doorbell: that's exactly what costs time many businesses don't have on site. A tailor-made CRM solution brings customer and contact data, visits, leads and orders into one place, cleanly maintained and available without detours.

## What the CRM takes care of

- **Customer and contact management**: A clean, current data set instead of scattered spreadsheets and business cards. Data quality that still holds up years later.
- **Visits and appointments**: Plan, document on the spot, and follow up, without re-entering everything in the evening.
- **Leads and sample orders**: New contacts and sample requests go straight into the system, no notes or extra step.
- **Revenue at a glance**: A simple report shows, before every visit, what a customer has bought over the past months and years.
- **Handoff to the ERP**: One click turns a visit report into a quote in the ERP, without retyping any data.

## Where to start?

Usually half a day is enough to sketch out customer and contact management, visit documentation and the ERP connection for quotes. From there, it's weeks, not months, to production.
