Taking measurements often still means a folding rule, a notepad, photos, and typing it all into the system again in the evening. Between the site and the quote sits a step that adds nothing. The room scanner on the platform shortens that path.

## How it works

- **Scan on site**: You capture the room with the LiDAR sensor of an iPad or iPhone. No special device, no extra tripod.
- **Take over the dimensions**: The scan produces areas, lengths and quantities, directly in the application.
- **Create the quote**: The quantities feed into the costing. Line items and prices are ready before you leave the site.

## Why it matters day to day

The biggest gain is not the minute saved while measuring. It is the re-entry that disappears. Finishing the quote on site gets you to a yes sooner. And transposed digits between notepad and system never happen in the first place.

## Where to start?

Take a type of object that comes up often in your work. One room type, one standard service. Once the flow works there, it extends to further cases.
