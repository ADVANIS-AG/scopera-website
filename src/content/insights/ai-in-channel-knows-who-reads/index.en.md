In the project channel someone asks: “How many open quotes does this customer have?” The answer arrives quickly. But the channel also includes someone from customer service who may not see quotes at all. An AI that simply answers has just bypassed a permission, and nobody noticed.

## What applies in the channel

- **AI on call**: “@AI” brings the SCOPERA AI into the channel. It can call on a data agent that searches, counts and summarises records but changes nothing.
- **The answer knows its audience**: If it touches data not everyone in the channel may see, only you see the answer. Whether it reaches everyone via “Share in channel” is your decision.
- **Stoppable at any time**: “@AI stop” cancels running tasks.
- **A daily budget**: Each channel has a daily AI budget. That keeps costs predictable.
- **Building only with the right and as a draft**: Only people with build rights can build apps in a channel, and that always produces a draft first.

## The same rules everywhere

What your organisation sets per role, meaning which rows and which fields someone sees, now applies everywhere: in the assistant, on module pages, in link pickers, in history, in the trash and on the start page. A picker for a module someone may not read stays empty. Some members therefore see less than before. That is not a bug but the rule now applying without gaps.

That is the difference from a chat window that sits next to the systems. An AI working inside the same system knows its rights. More on the [Governance](/en/governance/) page.

## Where to start?

Pick a channel with people in different roles and ask a question about your data there. Pay attention to who sees the answer.
