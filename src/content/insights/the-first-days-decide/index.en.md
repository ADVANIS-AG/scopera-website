The platform is set up, the logins have been sent. Then someone signs in for the first time, sees an empty start page and closes the window again. Whether new software takes hold in everyday work is rarely decided in the training session. It is decided in the first days afterwards, when nobody is sitting next to you.

## What new members can expect

- **One question to start**: Anyone new to a tenant sees a welcome window asking what SCOPERA should be used for.
- **Four stations**: On the start page, “Getting started” then leads through the platform in four stations, with short tours instead of long manuals.
- **Sample data to try out**: Anyone who wants to test something without touching real data uses sample data. It can be removed again.
- **A path of its own for admins**: Administrators set up different things than members and therefore get a path of their own.
- **No empty chat window**: In an empty chat, the AI assistant suggests suitable first questions.

## And those who are already on board?

Anyone who knows the platform hides the guided start and brings it back from the Help Center when needed. That is also where the new category “Getting started” lives. So it gets in the way of nobody who does not need it, and stays within reach for anyone returning after a break.

You recognise good solutions by users not wanting to work without them after a week. That week starts with the first login. What else the platform includes is shown on the [Product](/en/product/) page, and you can try it out in the [Playground](/en/playground/).

## Where to start?

Sign in with a test account as if you were new and walk through the four stations yourself. Wherever you get stuck, your staff will too.
