In the project channel someone writes: "How many complaints did we have last month, and can we build an overview for them?" That is two tasks: an analysis and a build request. The [first article on AI in the channel](/en/insights/ai-in-channel-knows-who-reads/) was about who sees an answer. This one is about who does the work.

## Three roles, clearly separated

- **SCOPERA AI**: It leads, answers questions and splits a request into up to three tasks that run one after the other.
- **Data agent**: It searches, counts and summarizes. It only reads and changes nothing.
- **Build agent**: It creates drafts for apps and changes, through the AI App Builder. The result is always a draft, and only people with build permissions can call on it.

The platform defines the agents. Tenants don't create their own, they can only adjust the display names.

## Who is responsible for what

- **The permissions of the person who gave the task**: Every task runs with the permissions of the person who gave it.
- **Only that person confirms**: In the channel, the AI carries out changes only after confirmation at most, even if the write mode otherwise allows "Automatic".
- **One card instead of many messages**: A task card shows which task is waiting, running or needs confirmation. The person who gave the request can cancel it at any time.
- **Files only on request**: The AI reads a file only if the message carrying the file addresses it explicitly. Anything else in the history stays unread.

## Where to start?

Give a small request in the channel that has two steps: analyze something and turn it into a suggestion. Watch the task card to see who takes on what.
