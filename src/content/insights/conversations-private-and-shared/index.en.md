Plenty of questions for an AI are ones nobody likes to ask in front of the whole team. What exactly does this figure mean? How do I word this rejection more politely? If such questions can only be asked in the group chat, they often go unasked. And anyone who moves to a separate tool for them carries the answer back by copy and paste.

## What “Conversations” brings together

- **One list for everything**: AI, people and channels sit in one place, with the filters “All”, “Private with AI”, “People” and “Channels” and one shared search. Separate pages for chat and assistant are gone.
- **Private with AI**: With “New conversation” you ask the AI in your own words. A private conversation is visible only to you and the AI.
- **Share what holds up**: An answer that has proven itself goes into a channel as a card via “Share …”. The discussion continues there, with the result rather than the whole path to it.
- **Clear boundaries to the outside**: Channels with support and with providers have no AI in them. People talk to people there.

## Why the order matters

A team learns faster when individuals can try things undisturbed and what works becomes visible afterwards. The private question lowers the threshold. The shared card makes sure good answers do not stay buried in a single thread. Both happen in the same place, without switching tools.

Whether your organisation already uses “Conversations” is set by ADVANIS per tenant. How the team then builds together is described in [“Ask first, then build”](/en/insights/team-chat-with-ai/).

## Where to start?

Take the question you have already been asked twice this week. Ask the AI privately, check the answer and share it as a card in the right channel.
