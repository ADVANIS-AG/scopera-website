An employee wants to know what is stored about her. A former colleague wants his account gone. And nobody knows exactly how long team chat messages are actually kept. Questions like these often reach IT by e-mail and wait there. Yet the revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection sets clear expectations: access as a rule within 30 days (Art. 25 FADP), handover in a common electronic format (Art. 28 FADP) and no retention beyond the purpose (Art. 6 para. 4 FADP).

## What each person does themselves

- **Download data**: Under “Profile” in the user menu, anyone can download a JSON file of everything SCOPERA stores about their account via “Download data” under “My data”.
- **Delete account**: Under “Delete account”, they remove their account themselves. This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. If you are the last person administering a tenant, hand the role over first.

## What belongs to the organisation

Content in workspaces, meaning records, chats and conversations, belongs to the organisation. It is not in the file and does not disappear with the account. That boundary is intended: personal account data belongs to the person, shared work stays with the team.

## Retention periods instead of endless storage

Under Administration → “Retention periods”, administrators decide how long team chat messages are kept. If left empty, it is 365 days; after that the messages are deleted for good. Appointments are now personal by default too, and only visible if they have a business link or have been shared.

These functions take work off your hands but do not replace legal advice. How we otherwise protect data, access and AI calls is described on the [Governance](/en/governance/) page.

## Where to start?

Download your own data once and look inside. Then set a retention period for team chat that you can justify.
