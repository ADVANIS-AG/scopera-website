A general chat assistant can do a remarkable amount. It explains, drafts, writes code. One thing it cannot do is bring your team to the same state. Everyone runs their own conversation, with their own context and their own result. In the end there are five variants and none of them runs.

## What building together adds

- **One shared state**: Everyone works on the same application. What one person changes, the others see.
- **The result runs**: At the end there is an application on the platform, with roles, data and permissions. No snippet of code still looking for a home.
- **The context is already there**: The AI knows your data structure and your rules, because it works inside the same system.
- **Traceable**: Every change is logged and reversible.

## Why that makes the difference

A chat window is a tool for one person. A business application grows in a team. The difference is not the model. The difference is where the work happens.

## Where to start?

Take a process where several people already have a say today. That is exactly where the difference shows most clearly.
