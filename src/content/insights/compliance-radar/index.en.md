Regulations change, and they rarely change loudly. A new requirement from the health authority, an adjusted reporting duty, a deadline that now falls earlier. Finding out only when an inspection arrives is a problem.

## How the radar works

- **Watch**: An agent follows the sources that apply to your industry and your location.
- **Assess**: Only what affects your business is relevant. The rest gets filtered out.
- **Turn into tasks**: A requirement becomes a concrete task with an owner and a deadline.
- **Follow through**: Task management shows what is open and what is done, with a record.

## What the radar is not

It does not replace legal advice. It makes sure nothing slips by unnoticed and that you can show what was done and when. The professional judgment stays with your people and your advisers.

## Where to start?

Take the area where you were last caught off guard. That is where the benefit shows fastest.
