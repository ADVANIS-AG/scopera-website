Every construction site knows the picture: equipment moves between the yard, the truck and the site, sometimes officially, sometimes because someone just needed it. The spreadsheet in the office knows nothing about it. Anyone looking for a machine calls around or drives to the yard to check in person.

That costs more than time. Equipment nobody can find gets bought or rented again, even though a unit is sitting somewhere already. And maintenance dates that live in no system get forgotten, until the machine breaks down on site.

## One code, three functions

A QR code on the equipment costs little and needs no special hardware. Any smartphone on site can read it. That solves three problems at once:

- **Location at a glance**: scan a piece of equipment and see immediately where it was last logged and who currently has it.
- **Rentals without paperwork**: check-out and check-in run through the same scan. Whoever takes a machine is logged, return date included.
- **Service reminders instead of luck**: operating hours or inspection intervals are stored against the machine. Before maintenance or a recurring inspection is due, the reminder arrives on its own, not only once something breaks.

## Where to start?

Not with the entire fleet at once. A sensible start is the equipment that moves most often or costs the most when it fails. A handful of QR labels, an app on the phones your team already carries, done. The rest of the fleet follows once the first pieces of equipment have shown the benefit.
