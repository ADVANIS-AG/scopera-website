The customer meeting is in Outlook, the visit report in SCOPERA, and in between someone types the date and time by hand. The obvious fix would be to synchronise everything: calendar, mail, attachments. But copying the whole mailbox into another platform creates a second store for confidential content that nobody asked for.

## What the connection takes over

- **Appointments automatically**: Under “Connections” you connect your Outlook calendar. With “Import appointments from Outlook” your appointments arrive in SCOPERA every 15 minutes, provided your e-mail address belongs to an employee entry.
- **Private appointments only on request**: They are imported only if your organisation allows it and you switch it on yourself.
- **An inbox without content**: The “Inbox” shows sender, subject and time of your own mail, never the content. “Open in Outlook” leads straight to the message.

## An appointment belongs to a person

An appointment is now personal by default. Colleagues only see it if it is linked to a customer, lead, opportunity or quote, or was explicitly shared with the department. An appointment marked private in Outlook always stays private. If you see fewer of other people's appointments than before, you see the intent behind it: what gets shared is what has a business link.

This requires your organisation to have set up the Microsoft connection. Which other systems can be connected is listed on the [Product](/en/product/) page.

## Where to start?

Connect only the calendar at first, and in the first week link every customer appointment to the right customer. Then the team sees exactly the appointments it needs to work together.
